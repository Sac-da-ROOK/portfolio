I spent the weekend preparing for my latest tournament and, as always, the emotional side of competition mattered as much as the calculation.

The opening position was quiet, but the middlegame became sharp quickly. I had to balance patience with initiative, and that tension shaped a lot of my choices.

## What I learned

- Slow down when the board is calm.
- Trust my preparation rather than forcing a tactical shot.
- Leave the result behind and review the decision-making process.

> The real value of a tournament is not just the score. It is the evidence of what you can improve next time.

I ended the event with more questions than certainties, which is a good sign. The next tournament will begin with a clearer plan and a calmer mindset.
